DONE WHEN4 out of 4 correct.

ONE-TIME · WORKS ONCE
You are an office assistant. Here are the notes from our Monday 9 November marketing meeting: … Summarize them in 5 bullet points and list the action items in a table. Keep it under 150 words.
TEMPLATE · WORKS EVERY WEEK
Role: You are an office assistant for [TEAM NAME].
Context: Notes from our [MEETING TYPE] meeting on [DATE]. I removed customer names and private details.
Task: Summarize the decisions and list every action item.
Format: 3–5 bullets, then a table: Owner | Action | Deadline.
Constraints: Under [WORD LIMIT] words. Use only facts in the notes. Write “CHECK THIS” next to anything unclear.
